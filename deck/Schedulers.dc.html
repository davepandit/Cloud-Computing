The Three Schedulers

Same servers, three different habits

round-robin
the naive baseline everyone starts with
every server, always on
Never migrates. Hands every new task to the next server in line, no matter how busy it is. Spreads load thin, forever — nobody ever gets to sleep.
mbfd (plain)
tight packing, recomputed from zero every round
packed tight, but reshuffled
next round: reshuffled again
Packs tight, but thrashes. Ignores where tasks already were and re-solves from scratch — so tasks jump servers even when nothing needed to change.
mbfd-migration
our novelty — tight packing that stays put
packed tight, same as before
next round: barely moved
Packs tight, and stays. A task only moves if the energy saved is worth more than the disruption of moving it. Same result, far less churn.
