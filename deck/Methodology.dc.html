Methodology

How we got here, step by step

1Read the base paper, find the gap
ROA optimizes energy + temperature + carbon — but only once, on a fixed batch of tasks. It never revisits its own decision as load changes.
2Re-implement its equations as a standalone simulator
The same power, energy, and carbon formulas from the paper, rebuilt in Python, so our results are directly comparable to its own.
3Build three interchangeable schedulers
Round-robin, plain best-fit packing, and our migration-cost-aware version — all tested under identical synthetic workloads for a fair comparison.
4Run a controlled experiment sweep
500 to 3,000 concurrent tasks, mirroring the paper's own evaluation range, with automated tests locking in the findings.
5Validate on a real Kubernetes cluster
Port the exact same scheduler code, unmodified, onto a live 5-node cluster — to prove the result isn't just a simulator artifact.
