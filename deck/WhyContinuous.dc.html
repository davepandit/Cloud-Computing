Why A Continuous Loop

A one-shot decision goes stale the moment load shifts

One-shot — the base paper
t = 0 (decided once)
→
t = 1 (no one looking)
→
t = 2 (still frozen)

One server gets overloaded, another sits nearly empty — and nothing ever revisits the decision.

Continuous — ours
t = 0
→
t = 1 (re-checked)
→
t = 2 (re-checked again)

Load stays balanced across active servers, and the third server can stay off the whole time.

“It's the difference between seating a restaurant once at opening time, versus a host who keeps checking the floor all night.”
